## ZIPTV Pro 10.0 — rebuilt from the ground up

ZIPTV Pro 10 is a complete rebuild for speed and reliability. One app now adapts to your
device — TV and projector, phone and tablet, or Windows PC — with no switching between modes.

**Your activation code stays the same** when you update from version 9, so your
playlists and provider setup carry on. Favorites and local watch history from version 9
start fresh.

**10.0.4:**
- Smart TV page: opens instantly in Chrome with "Always use secure connections" turned on
  (it used to keep loading forever).

**10.0.3:**
- Smart TV page: much faster to start playing, smoother scrolling, a backdrop home screen and
  movie/series pages with artwork, plot and cast.
- Player: NOW, NEXT and LATER with the time above each show, scrollable on phones; a smaller
  channel panel that carries the LIVE badge.

**10.0.2:**
- **Cast to TV** from the player menu: Samsung/LG and other DLNA TVs, and Chromecast / Google TV.
  Your phone or PC becomes the remote (pause, seek, stop).
- **Smart TV access**: open the address shown in Settings > Network in your TV's web browser
  (Samsung, LG...) for Live TV, Movies, Series, Search and Continue Watching on the big screen.
  "Record on PC" in its player records on the PC running the app.
- **Update inside the app** on Android and Fire TV: the new version downloads and installs from
  the update banner, no browser needed.
- Episodes: season picker, episode artwork with progress, runtime and time left.
- Watch Together button back in the header; Settings gains stream format (TS/HLS), sleep timer,
  and on Windows run at startup / start minimized.

**10.0.1:** keeps your v9 activation code; movie and series pages show artwork behind the
title; the play button no longer covers the loading spinner; paired PC and phone share one
Continue Watching list; faster start-up on Windows.

### What's new
- Much faster start-up, channel switching and catalog sync, with a real progress bar
- New player: slim seek bar, NOW/NEXT guide, quick channel list, volume and More menu
- TV guide with a time bar and "now" line; reminders, catch-up and recordings
- Series play the next episode automatically, with an "Up next" countdown
- Watch Together: watch a movie in sync with friends on the same provider
- Corner mini player while you browse (and picture-in-picture on phones)
- Plain M3U playlist links are supported, as well as Xtream logins
- Better channel logos and a more accurate TV guide
- On Windows, video playback is built in — no separate VLC install needed

### Installing
- **Android / Fire TV:** v10 updates the existing app. If you're asked, allow the update.
- **Windows:** run the installer. Version 9 is removed automatically the first time v10 opens.
