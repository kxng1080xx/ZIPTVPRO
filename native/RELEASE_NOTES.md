## ZIPTV Pro 10.0 — rebuilt from the ground up

ZIPTV Pro 10 is a complete rebuild for speed and reliability. One app now adapts to your
device — TV and projector, phone and tablet, or Windows PC — with no switching between modes.

**Your activation code stays the same** when you update from version 9, so your
playlists and provider setup carry on. Favorites and local watch history from version 9
start fresh.

**10.5.3:**
- PC: going back from picture-in-picture no longer crashes the app.
- TV web page (port 8790): scroll with the mouse wheel, touch or click-and-drag; hovering highlights.

**10.5.2:**
- Live TV: search the channels in a category from the bar above the list.
- Settings: edit a playlist (pencil button) to rename it or change which categories load.
- Channels named like "WGN AMERICA HD | USA" show their real name instead of just "USA", and find their logos.

**10.5.1:**
- Adding a playlist: paste the link at the top; the name fills in with the server's address. After the
  login checks out, choose which Live TV, Movies and Series categories to keep.
- Playlist names show again in Settings (they all said "Playlist").
- Settings shows when your activation and each playlist's account expire.
- Fixed a crash when a provider lists the same movie or series twice.
- Loading no longer looks stuck: it shows how much has downloaded and retries a stalled download sooner.

**10.5.0:**
- Web tabs go truly fullscreen: when a site's video goes fullscreen, the whole app does too (no sidebar
  or toolbar; phones turn landscape). Back or Esc returns.
- TV: web tabs have on-screen back, forward, reload and a cursor/links switch (no Menu key needed).
  Press Up at the top of a page to reach them.
- TV: web pages scroll on sites that scroll an inner area instead of the whole page.
- PC: a splash shows the moment you open the app, and the window opens sooner.
- Home shows your channels about a second sooner after launch.

**10.4.1:**
- PC: web tabs now use Microsoft Edge's engine (built into Windows), so movie and streaming sites play
  (they got stuck on "Finding sources"). The installer is about 120 MB smaller.
- PC: ad blocker for web tabs works with the new engine.
- PC: no more crash when closing the app after opening a web tab.

**10.4.0:**
- Web tabs: add any website as its own tab from the ZIPTV Pro website (/connect). It shows in the sidebar
  with the site's icon and opens inside the app.
- Built-in ad blocker for web tabs, on by default (Settings > Ad blocker).
- TV remotes work web tabs two ways, set per tab: a cursor you move with the arrows, or jumping between links
  and buttons. Menu switches between them; Back goes back a page.
- New Series icon in the sidebar.

**10.3.2:**
- Live TV on a phone held sideways is laid out for the short screen: no header bar (the phone already shows
  the time), a slimmer side bar with Search and Watch Together, channels on the left and the player on the
  right with what's on now and next underneath it, instead of empty space.

**10.3.1:**
- Recordings play again (they failed with "Couldn't play this stream").
- When a stream fails, the player buttons no longer cover the message and its Try again button.

**10.3.0:**
- Picture in picture that works outside the app. PC: a small always-on-top video window you can drag
  anywhere (from the player's More menu, or just minimize ZIPTV Pro while watching). Android: press Home
  or switch apps while anything plays and it keeps going in a floating window.
- Upscaler (player More menu or Settings): a sharper picture on SD and 720p channels.
- PC: updates install themselves. The update downloads, ZIPTV Pro closes, installs and reopens.
- PC and TV: Live TV uses one panel like phones. Pick a category, then its channels slide in; Back returns.
- PC: rows on Home and movie pages get arrow buttons to scroll through them.

**10.2.3:**
- When a stream won't play you now see what's happening: "Loading…", then up to 4 automatic retries
  ("Retrying 2 of 4…"), then a clear message with Try again. Works for live TV, movies, series and recordings.
- A playlist added on the ZIPTV Pro website (/connect) shows up within seconds and the app switches to it.
- The app checks for updates every 30 minutes while it's open, not only when it starts.
- PC: no longer offers an update you already installed.

**10.2.2:**
- Fire TV / Android TV: the remote works in the player again. Arrows bring up the controls and move
  between them (OK selects); they no longer switch channels. Use the on-screen channel buttons, or CH+/CH-.

**10.2.1:**
- Series pages no longer crash the app when the episodes load.
- Fixed a crash on series whose provider lists the same episode twice.

**10.2.0:**
- Movie playlists: put movies in your own playlist (e.g. every Transformers film) from a movie's page
  and they play back to back. Find them at the top of Movies and on Home; reorder or remove movies anytime.
- Similar movies on every movie page: the rest of its series first (all the X-Men films), then films like it,
  only ones your provider has.
- Tap an actor to see all their movies your provider has.
- New Home: a rotating spotlight of what you were watching with its artwork, a livelier live-channel card,
  and Top rated rows.
- Sidebar shows icons and opens with labels when you point at it; Settings sits at the bottom.
- PC: the Smart TV address is back at the top, and a new button turns on "Watch away from home" with a QR
  code to scan on your phone (also in Settings).
- No more flash of the activation code when the app starts; you see the logo while it loads.
- Player: the NOW / NEXT / LATER bar sits on a dark backing so it is easy to read.

**10.1.0:**
- PC: watch away from home. Settings > Network > "Watch away from home" gives a private link to your
  library that opens in any browser, on any network, while ZIPTV Pro is open on the PC.
- PC: the Home screen is no longer empty when your last watch was a live channel.
- Scheduled recordings work on one-stream plans, and a failed recording now says why.

**10.0.5:**
- PC: video shows again (since 10.0.0 the installed app played sound over a black screen).

**10.0.4:**
- Smart TV page: opens instantly in Chrome with "Always use secure connections" turned on
  (it used to keep loading forever).

**10.0.3:**
- Smart TV page: much faster to start playing, smoother scrolling, a backdrop home screen and
  movie/series pages with artwork, plot and cast.
- Player: NOW, NEXT and LATER with the time above each show, scrollable on phones; a smaller
  channel panel that carries the LIVE badge.

**10.0.2:**
- **Cast to TV** from the player menu: Samsung/LG and other DLNA TVs, and Chromecast / Google TV.
  Your phone or PC becomes the remote (pause, seek, stop).
- **Smart TV access**: open the address shown in Settings > Network in your TV's web browser
  (Samsung, LG...) for Live TV, Movies, Series, Search and Continue Watching on the big screen.
  "Record on PC" in its player records on the PC running the app.
- **Update inside the app** on Android and Fire TV: the new version downloads and installs from
  the update banner, no browser needed.
- Episodes: season picker, episode artwork with progress, runtime and time left.
- Watch Together button back in the header; Settings gains stream format (TS/HLS), sleep timer,

**10.0.1:** keeps your v9 activation code; movie and series pages show artwork behind the

Continue Watching list; faster start-up on Windows.

### What's new
- Much faster start-up, channel switching and catalog sync, with a real progress bar
- New player: slim seek bar, NOW/NEXT guide, quick channel list, volume and More menu
- TV guide with a time bar and "now" line; reminders, catch-up and recordings
- Series play the next episode automatically, with an "Up next" countdown
- Watch Together: watch a movie in sync with friends on the same provider
- Corner mini player while you browse (and picture-in-picture on phones)
- Plain M3U playlist links are supported, as well as Xtream logins
- Better channel logos and a more accurate TV guide
- On Windows, video playback is built in — no separate VLC install needed

### Installing
- **Android / Fire TV:** v10 updates the existing app. If you're asked, allow the update.
- **Windows:** run the installer. Version 9 is removed automatically the first time v10 opens.
